3. How to Cancel Your AutoPay Mandate Anytime (3 Easy Methods)

You have complete control over your subscription. You can cancel your AutoPay mandate at any time with zero cancellation penalties:

Method 1: Cancel Inside the AstraVeda Android App

  1. Open the AstraVeda AI application on your phone.
  2. Tap the Settings (⚙️) tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to "Subscription & AutoPay Terms".
  4. Tap "Cancel Subscription & AutoPay Mandate".
  5. Your subscription will be cancelled immediately and no further charges will occur.

Method 2: Cancel Directly Inside Your UPI App (Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm)

Since the mandate is registered with your UPI handle, you can cancel it directly from your payment app:

  • Google Pay: Tap your Profile picture (top right) > Autopay > Select AstraVeda AI / Razorpay > Tap Cancel Autopay > Enter UPI PIN to confirm.
  • PhonePe: Tap your Profile picture > AutoPay Settings > Select AstraVeda AI / Razorpay > Tap Remove AutoPay > Confirm with PIN.
  • Paytm: Tap Profile (top left) > UPI & Payment Settings > UPI Automatic Payments > Select the active mandate > Tap Cancel Automatic Payment.
