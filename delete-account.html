✦ Users of AstraVeda AI have the fundamental right to request complete and permanent deletion of their account, personal profile, and associated astrological consultation records at any time with zero hassle.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account and data deletion through either of the following straightforward methods:

Option A: Via Direct Email (Recommended)

Send an email from your registered email address to developer Gulshan Yadav at: gulshanyadav62000@gmail.com

  • Subject: AstraVeda AI Account and Data Deletion Request
  • Include: Your registered email ID or phone number used during sign-up.
  • Response Time: Your request will be acknowledged and processed within 24 to 48 business hours.
Option B: In-App Request

Open the AstraVeda AI app, navigate to Settings > Help Center & Contact Support > Request Data Deletion, or simply tap "Sign Out of AstraVeda" to discard local session cache.

2. What Data is Permanently Deleted

Upon receiving your deletion request, the following information will be permanently expunged from our active databases:

  • User Profile: Name, email address, profile photo, and OAuth user IDs.
  • Birth Chart Information: Date of birth, time of birth, place/city of birth, and calculated Janam Kundli planetary charts.
  • AI Chat Transcripts: All historical astrological inquiries, AI horoscope discussions, and remedy recommendations.
  • Biometric / Palmistry Records: Any temporary image landmarks processed during palmistry scans.

3. Data Retention & Exceptions

  • Tax & Legal Compliance: Records of financial payment transactions (such as Razorpay receipt IDs and order IDs) may be retained strictly as mandated by Indian tax, GST, and banking regulations for statutory audit purposes.
  • Permanent Purge: All personal identifiers linked to such transaction logs are anonymized immediately upon account deletion.
